# Demo Scope

## Implemented synthetic journey

The only scenario is a privately owned, Karnataka-registered, non-transport synthetic projection `KA01AB1234`, from synthetic seller Ananya Rao to synthetic buyer Rahul Shetty.

1. Shared-password sign-in to a fixed fictional seller account, followed by seller readiness/declaration.
2. Seller invites the fixed buyer; buyer reviews deterministic synthetic information/document checklist and makes a prototype declaration.
3. A server-owned labelled synthetic price creates a payment and leased mock-provider outbox attempt. No card, bank, or official-fee data is collected.
4. A confirmed synthetic payment lets the buyer submit a synthetic application. A mock government-submit outbox may acknowledge it with a synthetic external reference; timeout leaves it submitted and retryable, malformed replies quarantine the outbox.
5. An active `DEMO_OPERATOR`, only with `DEMO_MODE=true`, may dispatch eligible mock outbox work and emit derived, state-appropriate mock RTO events. Citizen accounts cannot access this page or use operator controls.
6. The ordered mock inbox can inward a sent case, request/acknowledge a targeted correction, approve/reject it, and mark registry completion. It can also confirm a pending withdrawal or demonstrate the inwarding/withdrawal race.
7. Exact seller/buyer participants can resume their protected case. Corrections use bounded fixed synthetic values; no real upload or identifier entry exists. Withdrawal is available only while submitted/sent; later states show mock manual-RTO guidance.

## Important boundaries

- This is not a VAHAN/RTO, government identity, payment, or registry service.
- Mock acknowledgement/reference, approval, correction, withdrawal, and registry-completion states are not official results or receipts.
- Approval does not mutate ownership. Only a valid matching mock registry-completion event changes the local synthetic ownership intervals.
- The operator simulator is a guarded test tool, not an officer workflow. It derives references/sequences server-side, has persisted cooldown/audit records, and accepts no arbitrary client state or reference.
- English and Kannada are implemented across the current citizen and operator surfaces. A live public-page pass verified persisted Kannada language state, `html[lang=kn]`, skip-link focus, and no horizontal overflow at 320px; the protected database-backed journey still requires full browser E2E in a seeded PostgreSQL environment.

## Explicitly out of scope

- Every live government, identity, payment, registry, financier, inspection, or notification integration.
- Real documents, Aadhaar/PAN-like values, bank/card details, legal advice, official fees, eligibility, times, or official receipts.
- Any second transport service, public callback endpoint, or real RTO back-office.

## Reusable synthetic workspaces

The default instant demo remains available. A citizen member may create a fresh isolated synthetic workspace or reset the selected workspace only; these commands recreate deterministic sample data and never claim an external or government reset.
